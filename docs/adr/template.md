# NNNN. Title in the imperative, naming the decision

Status: Proposed
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, and what we knew
when we decided. Write it so a reader a year from now understands why
this needed deciding at all.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two sentences first, then the detail. Use
"Galleon will…" or "We will…".

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. Include the costs we accept
and anything this decision rules out.

## Alternatives considered

Each option we rejected, and why, in a sentence or two.

## Sources

Links to the discussion, issue, PR or prior art behind the decision.
